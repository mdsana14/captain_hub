Captain Hub

The toolkit a student captain uses to share the workshop, and the dashboard that shows whether the campaign is on pace for 500 registrations.

Prototype with sample data. Names, colleges and counts below are made up. Nothing is connected to a real form or sent anywhere, and no results are real.

1. Get your code and link

Each captain gets one unique code. Students who register through the captain's link carry that code into the form, so every registration is credited to the right person.

Your code

With a real Google Form, use Get pre-filled link, type XXXX in the referral field, and paste the entry ID shown in that link above.

2. Share it where you already talk to people

Post once in each group you belong to and message a few friends directly. Do not add people to groups without asking, and do not send repeated reminders to the same group.
